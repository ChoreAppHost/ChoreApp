What Chorely does

A system, not a to-do list.

Every chore carries a priority, a schedule, and a point value — and it only pays out once someone else verifies the work. The busywork becomes a game worth playing, honestly.

Priority & points

Every chore is worth something.

Set each chore Low, Medium, or High. The harder the work, the more it’s worth, so effort is visible instead of assumed.

Low10 pts
Medium20 pts
High30 pts
Verified completion

No one grades their own work.

Finish a chore and it waits as pending until a partner, roommate, or parent approves it. Points only land on honest work.

Modes

Solo, couple, roommates, family.

Pick how your home runs. Parents mint a private code so a kid logs into their own view with just their chores.

Community points

Your points lift the whole home.

Points you earn count for you and for the household’s shared total, so the effort adds up for everyone.

Rewards catalog

Rewards you write yourself.

You define every perk and its point cost: a movie pick, a lie-in, skipping a chore, whatever motivates your home. Kids request; a parent approves.

Three moves

How it works.

1

Add the chores

Drop the household’s recurring jobs onto one roster. Set a priority, a date window, and who’s responsible.

2

Finish & verify

Check a chore off and it goes pending. Someone else approves it — then the points land on your balance and the shared total.

3

Redeem together

Spend points on the rewards your household set for itself. Everyone wins from the same effort.

The reward system

You make the rewards.

Chorely keeps score. What the points are worth is up to your household: write your own perks, set their point cost, and change them whenever you like.
